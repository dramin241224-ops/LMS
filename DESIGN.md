# Design System: Primary English Quest

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Countryside Explorer Passport"**

A sunlit, tactile learning surface designed for Vietnamese Grade 2 English learners and primary teachers. The system treats vocabulary mastery as physical ink stamps collected along a countryside journey.

### Key Characteristics:
- High-contrast, friendly rounded typography (Fredoka display + single-story Lexend body).
- Warm parchment field palette (`#FBF6E8`) with vivid meadow green (`#1F7E37`), river azure (`#1864B8`), and vermilion stamp ink (`#C8351B`).
- Dual-distance ergonomics: legible from the back of a sunlit classroom TV and responsive for 7-year-old fingers on a touch tablet.
- 100% offline self-contained delivery with embedded WOFF2 fonts and authored SVGs.

## Colors

### Primary
- **Meadow Green** (`#1F7E37`): Primary forward actions, success confirmations, and unit badges.

### Secondary
- **River Azure** (`#1864B8`): Audio pronunciation triggers, listening mode highlights, and word assembly slots.

### Accent
- **Vermilion Stamp Red** (`#C8351B`): Phonics `/r/` sound indicators, onset letter highlights, and ink seals.
- **Sunshine Gold** (`#FFB800`): Active trail stops, star counters, and reward badges.

### Neutral
- **Map Parchment** (`#FBF6E8`): Main stage sheet background.
- **Paper White** (`#FFFDF8`): Card surfaces and interactive tiles.
- **Warm Sand Border** (`#D4C096`): Grounded structural borders and dividers.
- **Deep Pine Ink** (`#1B3124`): High-contrast primary reading text.

## Typography

- **Display Font:** Fredoka (embedded WOFF2)
- **Body Font:** Lexend (embedded WOFF2)

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Meadow green with deep green border and soft drop shadow.
- **Phonics / Audio Trigger:** Vermilion or River Blue with speaker icon.

### Cards
- **Landmark Tile:** White paper surface, 3px sand border, rounded 26px corners.
- **Ink Stamp:** Tilted vermilion badge with star icon and high-contrast white text.
